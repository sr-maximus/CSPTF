# Changelog

## [0.1.1-draft] - 2026-08-03

### Added

- Professional CSPTF operating architecture diagram.
- Public architecture and tooling documentation pages.
- Tooling and evidence guidance in English and Spanish.
- Tooling register with official references, applicable domains and evidence limits.
- Profile-tailored evidence matrix generator.
- AP2 evidence matrix fixture aligned to the generated AP2 checklist.
- Safe fictional evidence record and test record examples.

### Changed

- Catalog validation now checks research source references, claim references and tooling-register integrity.
- Unit tests now verify tooling coverage across all CSPTF domains and AP2 evidence-matrix scope.

## [0.1.0-draft] - 2026-07-31

### Added

- CSPTF charter, scope, lifecycle, Rules of Engagement and ethical safeguards.
- Twenty-domain architecture.
- 160 draft controls.
- 240 draft authorized test cases.
- 100 threat scenarios and 100 weakness patterns.
- Four assurance profiles and six evidence levels.
- Draft crypto-economic and systemic risk model.
- Domain crosswalks and research source register.
- Machine-readable JSON/CSV catalogs, schemas and validators.
- Assessment, finding, report, retest and disclosure templates.
- Spanish paper, consolidated specification and release validation material.

### Known limitations

- Risk weights and thresholds are not empirically calibrated.
- Mappings are thematic unless explicitly identified as verified.
- English and Spanish normative text require expert harmonization.
- No certification program exists.
