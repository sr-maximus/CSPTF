# Code of Conduct

CSPTF follows a professional, evidence-centered and respectful collaboration model.

Participants must:

- engage constructively and avoid harassment or discrimination;
- distinguish fact, inference, opinion and uncertainty;
- respect confidentiality, authorization boundaries and responsible disclosure;
- avoid publishing operational details that create unnecessary risk to live systems;
- disclose material conflicts of interest;
- give accurate attribution.

Maintainers may edit, reject or remove contributions that violate safety, legality, privacy, quality or community standards. Serious incidents may be reported through the private process in `SECURITY.md`.
