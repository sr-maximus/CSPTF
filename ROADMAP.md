# Roadmap

## v0.1 - Foundation (current)

- charter, scope, lifecycle and Rules of Engagement;
- 20 domains;
- 160 controls, 240 tests, 100 threats and 100 weaknesses;
- draft risk and evidence models;
- initial crosswalks, templates, schemas and validators;
- Spanish paper and publication package.

## v0.2 - Review and translation

- English normative translation;
- expert review panels for smart contracts, custody, DeFi, bridges, CEX and infrastructure;
- validated AADAPT/ATT&CK technique-level mappings;
- test selection decision trees;
- public case-study templates and pilot assessments.

## v0.5 - Calibration

- inter-rater reliability study;
- risk-weight calibration against historical incidents;
- coverage benchmarking;
- reference implementation of an assessment workspace;
- STIX 2.1 export profile.

## v1.0 - Stable standard candidate

- stable identifiers and conformance model;
- independent editorial and legal review;
- full public comment resolution;
- reproducible release artifacts and DOI;
- no certification claim unless separate governance is approved.

## Future

- chain-specific annexes;
- mobile/hardware-wallet annex;
- ZK and privacy-protocol annex;
- automated evidence adapters;
- safe attack-emulation lab;
- multilingual documentation.
