# CSPTF-THRT-DEF-003 - Ataque de liquidez temporal

- **Domain:** DEF
- **Status:** draft

## Scenario

Actor externo, interno o automatizado intenta provocar ataque de liquidez temporal para obtener acceso, control, beneficio económico, fraude, interrupción o ventaja estratégica.

## Assets at risk

pools; AMM; lending markets; vaults

## AADAPT tactic alignment

Execution; Impact; Fraud

## Modeling prompts

- actor capability and access;
- economic or strategic incentive;
- prerequisites and timing;
- chain/off-chain path;
- composability and contagion;
- prevention, detection, containment and recovery;
- evidence that would confirm or refute the scenario.
