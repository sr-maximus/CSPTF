# CSPTF-THRT-REG-005 - Retención o uso incompatible de datos

- **Domain:** REG
- **Status:** draft

## Scenario

Actor externo, interno o automatizado intenta provocar retención o uso incompatible de datos para obtener acceso, control, beneficio económico, fraude, interrupción o ventaja estratégica.

## Assets at risk

PII; KYC records; transaction monitoring; sanctions screening

## AADAPT tactic alignment

Collection; Defense Evasion; Impact; Fraud

## Modeling prompts

- actor capability and access;
- economic or strategic incentive;
- prerequisites and timing;
- chain/off-chain path;
- composability and contagion;
- prevention, detection, containment and recovery;
- evidence that would confirm or refute the scenario.
