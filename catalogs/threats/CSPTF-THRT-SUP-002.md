# CSPTF-THRT-SUP-002 - Compromiso de repositorio o maintainer

- **Domain:** SUP
- **Status:** draft

## Scenario

Actor externo, interno o automatizado intenta provocar compromiso de repositorio o maintainer para obtener acceso, control, beneficio económico, fraude, interrupción o ventaja estratégica.

## Assets at risk

source repositories; dependencies; package registries; compilers

## AADAPT tactic alignment

Resource Development; Initial Access; Execution; Defense Evasion; Impact

## Modeling prompts

- actor capability and access;
- economic or strategic incentive;
- prerequisites and timing;
- chain/off-chain path;
- composability and contagion;
- prevention, detection, containment and recovery;
- evidence that would confirm or refute the scenario.
