# 00 - Charter

## Mission

CSPTF provides a repeatable, authorized and evidence-driven method to evaluate security across the complete digital-asset system, not only smart-contract source code.

## Objectives

- unify technical, economic, operational and governance testing;
- establish stable identifiers and machine-readable catalogs;
- require explicit authorization and safe execution;
- make evidence quality and uncertainty visible;
- support scoping for diverse architectures and chains;
- map rather than duplicate specialized standards;
- improve comparability across assessments without erasing context.

## Non-objectives

CSPTF is not:

- legal authorization;
- a guarantee of security, solvency or regulatory compliance;
- an exploit cookbook for live systems;
- a substitute for cryptographic proof, formal verification or financial audit;
- a certification program in v0.1;
- limited to EVM or public blockchains.

## Unit of assessment

The unit is the **digital-asset system**, represented as a graph of assets, actors, trust boundaries, components, flows of value, states, external dependencies and recovery mechanisms.

## Quality properties

A conforming assessment is:

- authorized;
- scoped;
- threat-informed;
- reproducible;
- evidence-backed;
- risk-contextual;
- safe and reversible where possible;
- explicit about limitations;
- independently reviewable;
- closed through remediation and retest.
