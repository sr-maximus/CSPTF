# 01 - Scope and taxonomy

## System layers

CSPTF models ten interacting layers:

1. business, governance and regulation;
2. economic design and market behavior;
3. identity, custody and signing;
4. application and smart-contract logic;
5. protocol, consensus and cross-chain communication;
6. APIs, clients and user interfaces;
7. infrastructure, cloud and supply chain;
8. telemetry, fraud and threat intelligence;
9. incident response, recovery and reserves;
10. third-party and ecosystem dependencies.

## Scope object

The scope record MUST include:

- legal owner and authorizing authority;
- assessment objective;
- in-scope and explicitly out-of-scope assets;
- chains, networks, environments and contract addresses;
- source, bytecode, build and deployment versions;
- wallets, signers, custody models and limits;
- APIs, nodes, cloud accounts, domains and repositories;
- third parties and dependency boundaries;
- data classifications;
- test windows;
- forbidden techniques;
- transaction, gas and financial budgets;
- rollback and reconciliation plan;
- reporting and disclosure route.

## System archetypes

CSPTF can be tailored to:

- L1/L2 protocol;
- smart-contract protocol or dApp;
- DeFi protocol;
- bridge/interoperability service;
- centralized exchange or broker;
- custodian or wallet provider;
- token/NFT platform;
- institutional blockchain;
- validator or node operator;
- infrastructure or developer-platform provider.

## Tailoring rule

A domain MAY be marked not applicable only when the assessor documents:

1. why its assets and trust assumptions are absent;
2. which evidence supports that decision;
3. whether a third party carries the risk;
4. who approved the exclusion.
