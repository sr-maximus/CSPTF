# 10 - Reporting standard

## Executive report

- decision statement;
- scope and assurance profile;
- system and value-flow overview;
- maximum and concentrated risks;
- critical scenarios and business effect;
- strengths and limitations;
- prioritized actions, owners and time horizons;
- residual risk and retest plan.

## Technical report

- authorization and RoE;
- architecture and threat model;
- versioned asset inventory;
- test matrix;
- methods and tools;
- evidence register;
- detailed findings;
- risk calculations;
- detection/response results;
- remediation guidance;
- exclusions and constraints;
- appendices and hashes.

## Language rules

Reports distinguish:

- observed fact;
- reproduced behavior;
- inference;
- assumption;
- untested hypothesis.

Avoid unsupported absolutes such as “secure,” “unhackable,” or “fully compliant.”

## Sensitive distribution

Use role-based access, encryption, watermarks/classification where appropriate, controlled retention and a sanitized public summary for coordinated disclosure.
