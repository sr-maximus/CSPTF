# Mapping to MITRE AADAPT

MITRE AADAPT is the primary adversary-behavior reference for CSPTF. AADAPT describes tactics and techniques for digital-asset management systems and is modeled after ATT&CK.

CSPTF uses AADAPT to:

- seed threat scenarios;
- connect test coverage to adversary goals;
- design detection and response exercises;
- avoid conflating a weakness with adversary behavior.

CSPTF adds a testing/assurance layer: authorization, system decomposition, controls, evidence, risk, reporting, remediation and retest.

The v0.1 mapping is tactic-level. It must not be represented as a complete technique-by-technique crosswalk.
