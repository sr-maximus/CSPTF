# Mapping to NIST

## NIST SP 800-115

CSPTF adopts the discipline of planning, conducting, analyzing and mitigating technical assessment activity, then specializes it for digital assets through value-flow models, forks/testnets, transaction budgets, irreversible effects and economic/systemic impact.

## NIST CSF 2.0

Domains map to Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond and Recover. These mappings support program integration, not automatic conformance.

## NIST SSDF and cryptographic standards

CSPTF SUP/INF use SSDF themes for secure development and supply-chain integrity. CRY uses relevant NIST cryptographic standards as one source of approved primitives and lifecycle considerations.
