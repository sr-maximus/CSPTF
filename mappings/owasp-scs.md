# Mapping to OWASP Smart Contract Security

OWASP SCSVS, SCSTG and SCWE are authoritative inputs for smart-contract and dApp testing. CSPTF references rather than copies their detailed test cases.

Primary CSPTF domains:

- ARC - architecture and threat modeling;
- CRY - cryptography;
- SCT - smart-contract logic;
- DAP - dApp and wallet integration;
- TOK - token behavior;
- DEF - economic/business logic;
- ORA - oracle use;
- BRG - cross-contract and cross-chain interaction.

CSPTF extends beyond that center into custody, centralized exchanges, nodes, Layer 2, cloud, identity, compliance, monitoring, reserves and crisis recovery.
