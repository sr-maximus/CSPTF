# Regulatory and compliance mapping

CSPTF REG and IRR help collect technical evidence relevant to obligations such as AML/KYT, privacy, operational resilience and incident response.

References include FATF virtual-asset guidance, EU MiCA and DORA. Jurisdiction, legal interpretation and applicability remain the responsibility of qualified legal/compliance professionals.

A penetration test cannot by itself establish regulatory compliance, solvency or adequacy of reserves.
