# Research methodology

## Question

What publicly available standards, frameworks, specifications and research can support an end-to-end security testing framework for digital-asset systems, and what material coverage gaps remain?

## Source classes

1. primary standards and official specifications;
2. official threat knowledge bases;
3. peer-reviewed or openly archived academic research;
4. protocol improvement proposals;
5. regulation and intergovernmental guidance;
6. public incident research used to validate threat relevance.

## Inclusion criteria

- direct relevance to blockchain, digital assets, technical testing, assurance, cryptography, supply chain, resilience or regulation;
- identifiable author or issuing organization;
- stable publication record or official repository;
- actionable concepts that can be mapped to a domain, control, threat or test;
- preference for primary sources.

## Exclusion criteria

- anonymous marketing material;
- unsupported vulnerability lists;
- exploit instructions without defensible research value;
- sources whose content could not be verified;
- duplicated secondary summaries where a primary source exists.

## Synthesis method

Sources were coded into system layers, security objectives, adversary behavior, control properties, test methods, evidence, risk and governance. Overlap was preserved as crosswalks; gaps became CSPTF extensions.

## Limitations

This is a structured narrative review, not yet a registered systematic review. Search strings, screening counts and inter-reviewer agreement must be added before claiming PRISMA-style systematic-review rigor.
