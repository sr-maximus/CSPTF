# Validation plan

CSPTF is not validated merely because it is detailed. Validation requires multiple evidence streams.

## Stage 1 - Content validity

Recruit domain experts for smart contracts, custody, DeFi, bridges, CEX, infrastructure, cryptography, compliance and incident response. Measure agreement on relevance, clarity and completeness.

## Stage 2 - Mapping validity

Independently review mappings to AADAPT, ATT&CK, OWASP, EEA and NIST. Record disagreements and mapping confidence.

## Stage 3 - Case-study coverage

Apply CSPTF retrospectively to public incidents and prospectively to authorized test environments. Measure whether the framework identifies the causal path, affected controls, evidence and recovery gaps.

## Stage 4 - Inter-rater reliability

Two or more assessment teams independently scope and rate the same system. Calculate agreement for applicability, test outcomes, evidence level, confidence and risk band.

## Stage 5 - Scoring calibration

Compare draft impact/likelihood dimensions with historical loss, outage, user impact, contagion and recovery. Adjust weights and thresholds without hiding uncertainty.

## Stage 6 - Usability and efficiency

Measure time, evidence burden, missed high-risk paths, duplication and assessor feedback by profile.

## Acceptance criteria before v1.0

- public comment resolution;
- independent expert review across all critical domains;
- documented case studies;
- acceptable inter-rater reliability;
- calibrated risk model or removal of unsupported numeric precision;
- stable identifiers and migration policy;
- legal and safety review.
