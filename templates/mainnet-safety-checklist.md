# Active Production/Mainnet Safety Checklist

> Active production testing is exceptional.

- [ ] Static, local, fork, testnet and staging methods were insufficient.
- [ ] Business and technical reasons are documented.
- [ ] Exact CSPTF test IDs are authorized.
- [ ] Owner, legal authority and stop authority signed.
- [ ] No customer funds or identities are used.
- [ ] Synthetic test assets and accounts are isolated.
- [ ] Transaction, gas, rate, volume, time and loss budgets are explicit.
- [ ] Monitoring and emergency communications are live.
- [ ] Chain state, balances and dependencies are baselined.
- [ ] Rollback, compensating recovery and reconciliation are tested.
- [ ] Third-party and market effects are assessed.
- [ ] Forbidden variants are documented.
- [ ] Cleanup and post-test reconciliation are assigned.
- [ ] Incident conversion and disclosure routes are ready.
