## Prioritization rules

- eliminate shared root causes before treating symptoms;
- address unrestricted authority and irreversible loss paths first;
- include temporary containment and durable correction;
- account for upgrade, migration, governance and disclosure risk;
- schedule independent retest for Critical/Systemic items.
